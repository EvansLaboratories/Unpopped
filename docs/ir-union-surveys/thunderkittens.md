## 2. The 10 highest-impact gaps (ordered by how much of TK each blocks)

1. **No kernel-level body / tile-valued SSA.** Every IR node is a whole-tensor `OpDef` with one `Access`; TK is an imperative per-warp program over register/shared tiles with loops. Without a body IR with loops and tile values nothing below can even be written. (Absent: Tile=0 hits.)
2. **No memory-space classes** (register tile, shared tile with swizzle, tensor memory, DSMEM). swizzle/shared/TMEM = 0 hits.
3. **No scope attribute** (thread/warp/warpgroup/block/cluster/grid) and no warp-role predicates; TK's op semantics depend on the executing group.
4. **No barriers/mbarrier phases** (init/arrive/wait/expect_bytes, named barriers, fences, proxy fence). barrier = 0 hits.
5. **No async-copy/TMA family** (load, store, reduce-store, prefetch, multicast, commit/wait groups).
6. **MMA is a tensor-math node, not a tile instruction.** Missing: wgmma async token (fence/commit/wait<N>), operand-residency rules (A reg-or-shared, B shared), tcgen05 with TMEM operands, cta_group 2, transposes as flags, `AccumSpec` variants (only `WideFloat`).
7. **Software pipeline / producer-consumer (LCSF) and persistent grid.** Needs staged shared buffers, ring semaphores, role-conditioned regions.
8. **Block-scaled low precision:** no fp4 e2m1, no scaled-contraction (mxfp8/nvfp4); `F8E8M0` exists only as a dtype.
9. **Stateful tile reductions** (`row_max(dst, src, src_accum)`): loop-carried running max/sum is what online softmax is; IR has only whole-axis folds. Also per-row vector inputs to an epilogue (RowReduce "single input").
10. **Multi-GPU:** PGL, multicast pointers, `multimem.ld_reduce/red/st`, peer TMA. peer/multicast = 0 hits.

### What a TK attention kernel needs *beyond* an `Access::Attention` node
An `Attention{softmax:Online{kv_block}, mask}` node (design doc lines 215-226) captures the *semantics* of FA-style forward. A TK kernel (e.g. kernels/attention/mha_h100, mha_h100_lcf, bf16_b300_mha_*; the directories were listed, the kernels were NOT read, so every item below is inferred from the TK ops above and from the README's "FA3 with kittens" claim) additionally depends on:
- Q/K/V as shared tiles loaded by TMA with swizzle and semaphores, double/multi-buffered K/V stages (input_block ring);
- warpgroup-specialized producer (TMA issue) vs consumer warpgroups, with register reallocation between roles (UNVERIFIED in TK; FA3 does `setmaxnreg`; not seen in the headers I read);
- async wgmma for QK^T (A from registers/shared, B shared) and PV (P as a register A operand: requires the accumulator-to-A-operand layout conversion `swap_layout`/convert to bf16);
- running `row_max` / `row_sum` with `src_accum`, `exp2` with pre-scaled log2e, rescale of the output accumulator by `mul_row`;
- causal tril/neg_infty masking at diagonal tiles only (tile-relative coordinates);
- an O epilogue to shared then TMA store; backward additionally needs TMA `store_add_async` (reduce-store) for dQ and a different tile ordering.
So `Attention` as a semantic node leaves ALL of the cooperation unmodelled. Under the owner's ruling that the IR models cooperation, this node is the *intent* tier and the above is the *schedule/implementation* tier, which today has no IR at all (plan::Schedule has `RowReduce{block}` and `Contraction` with no payload, plan.rs:26-86).

## 3. Things in the IR that look wrong or misleading relative to ThunderKittens

1. **docs/idiom-lifting-design.md section 1a (lines ~69-83) states "the IR never models cooperation".** This is now contradicted by the owner's ruling and by TK, where cooperation *is* the semantics (scopes, barriers, TMA). It must be replaced, not caveated. Also lines ~715-720 list TMA/wgmma/clusters/warp-specialization as "Not transferable" to other targets: TK's ThunderMittens (8x8 simdgroup tiles) and HipKittens (CDNA MFMA tiles) show the *tile abstraction* transfers while the instructions do not; the IR should hold the abstraction and the registry should hold the per-target lowering [TM][HK] (basis for HK/TM is search snippets only).
2. **ir.rs:1672-1690 Contraction doc says "v1: rank-2 single-K dense row-major ... batch axes, transposes ... are growth axes" and `AxisRole::Batch` says "v1: unused (rank-2)" (ir.rs:1870), but `ContractionAxes::batched_matmul()` exists and is wired.** The docs lag the code; a reader sizing the TK gap from the doc over-counts the gap on batching.
3. **`AccumSpec` has one variant and its name `WideFloat` hides the wgmma/tcgen05 facts:** TK MMA ops accumulate in the instruction's own precision (fp16 or fp32 acc, fp32 only for fp8 on Hopper), with `mm_*` (D=) vs `mma_*` (D+=) as an *operation* distinction (warpgroup.cuh). `Contraction` has no "accumulate into existing D" flag, so K-split / multi-tile accumulation (the standard TK loop `for k: mma_AB(acc, a, b)`) cannot be expressed as a chain of Contractions without materializing acc. Treat `accumulate: bool` / initial-acc input as a missing field.
4. **Contraction v1 is "uniform dtype"** (user-provided fact); TK/wgmma/tcgen05 inputs A,B must be the *same* dtype but the accumulator and output differ (e.g. fp8 in, fp32 acc, bf16 out), and block-scaled forms add two scale operands of a third dtype. Uniform dtype cannot express even the plain bf16 x bf16 -> fp32 case.
5. **inf constants**: TK uses `neg_infty`/`pos_infty` fills as masks (maps.cuh). `ScalarExpr::Const(f64)` can hold infinity in memory, but git log shows 0.22.14 "unpopped-slang spells NaN/inf constants as Slang, not C macros" (commit 172d8eb), i.e. emitter spelling was a past defect class; any TK-lifted mask (`-inf` + `exp2`) hits it. Not verified for other backends.
6. **`Access::Scan`, `RowSort`, `Im2Col`, `Window` have no TK counterpart** (TK has no scan op in the headers I listed; its SSM/linear-attention kernels do chunked scans by hand with tile mma + decay masks). Not wrong, but the IR's nodes are shaped by elementwise/CUDA-baracuda needs, not by tiles; a TK-lifted kernel will not map onto them one-to-one.
7. **`ReduceOp` has `Mean` but no `LogSumExp`/online (max, sum) pair monoid.** TK's `row_max` then `exp2(x-max)` then `row_sum` with rescale is a *two-state* monoid; a single-state `ReduceOp` cannot hold it, which is why the design doc needs `softmax: Online{kv_block}` as its own numerics axis.
8. **View::Reshape is "carried for recognition + keying only" (ir.rs:1996)**; TK relies on sub-tile reinterpretation (`subtile<cols>` returning a true reference, st.cuh), which is a genuine address-math change. Same class.
