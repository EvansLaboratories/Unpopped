kind: **BREAKING**: `check_kernel` refuses a `Subgroup` collective after a lane-divergent `Return` (`KernelError::CollectiveAfterReturn`)
---
A `Return` under control that may differ between the lanes of one subgroup takes some lanes out; a `Subgroup`-scope `Collective` or `CollectiveTuple` after it ran over every lane in the oracle and over the lanes still active on a device, so it had no single meaning. `check_kernel` and `check_module` now refuse such a kernel with the new `KernelError::CollectiveAfterReturn` (the enum is `non_exhaustive`). **Behaviour change (the shape now refused):** `if (<lane-dependent condition>) return;` followed, anywhere later in the kernel body, by a `Subgroup` `Collective` or `CollectiveTuple`, with a lane-dependent condition being one that may differ between the lanes of a subgroup (an expression of `threadIdx.x`, the lane id, or a local derived from a load or a lane-dependent value). It was admitted before. **The shapes that stay admitted:** a `Return` whose condition is uniform across each subgroup (built only from `blockIdx`, `blockDim`, scalar parameters, constants, `tid >> log2(N)` or `tid / N` with `N` the one subgroup size the launch states, and `threadIdx.y`/`.z` only when the launch states one subgroup size that divides the block x extent), which is the `if (row >= nrows) return;` guard of the gguf mmvq kernels; a `Return` after the collective; a `Return` before a `Barrier` (unchanged); and a `Block`-scope collective (unchanged). Also, `threadIdx.y` and `.z` are now lane-uniform when the launch states one subgroup size that divides the block x extent, so `if (row >= nrows) return;` with `row` built from `threadIdx.y` is not refused, and a subgroup collective under `if (threadIdx.y == 0)` is admitted. `unpopped::fixtures::divergent_fixtures()` (feature `fixtures`) gives two kernels the checker refuses, each with its error. Board 506 (ROADMAP U63). Verified red first: the divergent kernels were admitted before the change; each refusal has its uniform control beside it.
