kind: **BREAKING**: `check_kernel` refuses a `Subgroup` collective after a lane-divergent `Return` (`KernelError::CollectiveAfterReturn`)
---
A `Return` under control that may differ between the lanes of one subgroup takes some lanes out; a `Subgroup`-scope `Collective` or `CollectiveTuple` after it ran over every lane in the oracle and over the lanes still active on a device, so it had no single meaning. `check_kernel` and `check_module` now refuse such a kernel with the new `KernelError::CollectiveAfterReturn` (the enum is `non_exhaustive`). **Behaviour change:** a kernel with an early `Return` by a subset of lanes before a subgroup collective was admitted before and is refused now. A `Return` under warp-uniform control (whole subgroups leave) is still admitted, and so is one before a `Barrier` (unchanged). Also, `threadIdx.y` and `.z` are now lane-uniform when the launch states one subgroup size that divides the block x extent, so `if (row >= nrows) return;` with `row` built from `threadIdx.y` is not refused, and a subgroup collective under `if (threadIdx.y == 0)` is admitted. `unpopped::fixtures::divergent_fixtures()` (feature `fixtures`) gives two kernels the checker refuses, each with its error. Board 506 (ROADMAP U63). Verified red first: the divergent kernels were admitted before the change; each refusal has its uniform control beside it.
