`docs/design-dynamic-layout.md` section 10 and `tests/kernel_module_layout.rs`: the oracle already applies an allocation's layout (static or the launch-bound `dyn_layout`) to a callee's accesses through `CallArg::Alloc` (measured by a fill-through-a-callee read-back over a non-identity layout, static and dynamic, and a refused bind trapping before the call), so the IR needs no change; the gap is cpu-c's affine `(ptr, shape, stride, base)` buffer ABI, which the design closes with layout parameters (a C struct of integers and XOR-bit constants, not a tensor descriptor) beside only those callee parameters that receive a laid-out allocation; refusals that stay and patent conditions listed. `docs/options-u55-scheduling-hints.md`: three carrier options for the non-semantic scheduling hints (PM Q14 A) with what each breaks, a recommendation (one `Stmt::ScheduleHint` with a `non_exhaustive` kind), the semantics, and the release.
