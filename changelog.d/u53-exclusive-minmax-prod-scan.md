kind: Added: exclusive `Max`/`Min`/`Prod` collective scans and a `Prod` fold, for the kernel-layer `Stmt::Collective` (U53, Slang `WavePrefixMin/Max/Product`)
---
Non-breaking (no new variant or field: `ReduceOp::Prod` already existed). `check_kernel` now admits `ReduceOp::Prod` for a collective `Reduce` or `Scan` (any integer, `F32`, `F64`), and an **exclusive** `Max`/`Min`/`Prod` scan, which it refused before. Lane 0 of an exclusive scan follows the exclusive `Mean` precedent (PM ruling Q12): a **float** lane 0 is NaN; an **integer** lane 0 is the combine's identity, the type's least value for `Max`, its greatest for `Min`, and 1 for `Prod` (the ruling says "the type's extreme", which has no reading for `Prod`; 1 is the identity of the combine). `Prod` on integers wraps; on floats it rounds once to the type. `unpopped-cpu-c` lowers all of it (NaN as the C `NAN`), and its differential against the oracle covers the new folds at `I32` and `F32`, block and subgroup scope. Tests: `tests/kernel_collective.rs` (hand-written expected arrays), `unpopped-cpu-c/tests/kernel_collective_emit_agrees.rs`; a seeded mutant of the `Max` identity turns the oracle test red. Not here: `And`/`Or`/`Xor` on floats, tuple collectives.
