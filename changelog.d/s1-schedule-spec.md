kind: Added: `unpopped::schedule`, a `ScheduleSpec` vocabulary and `check_schedule` (board 500 Q2, plan phase S1)
---
A schedule states how a kernel is run, never what it computes: the tile shape hierarchy (block, warp, instruction), the stage count, the work division (data-parallel, persistent, stream-K, split-K), the shared-memory carveout, the epilogue tile and the cluster shape. `check_schedule` refuses a spec that is not self-consistent (zero extents, tile levels that do not nest, a zero stage count, split-K of fewer than two slices, an epilogue sub-tile that does not divide its tile); `ScheduleSpec::set_fields` lists what a spec sets so an emitter honours each field or declines it by name. Additive: no field of `KernelFn` carries a spec yet (the carrier rides the plan breaking release B1) and the oracle ignores a schedule. `schedule_fixtures()` (feature `fixtures`) gives specs for a decline test. Plan: `docs/design-tile-and-schedule-plan.md`.
