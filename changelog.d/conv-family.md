`kernel_conv_family` (oracle, against a host scatter reference) and `kernel_conv_family_emit_agrees` (`unpopped-cpu-c` against the oracle): an NHWC convolution with stride (2, 1), dilation (1, 2), padding (1, 1), a non-square image and filter and taps off the image at both ends of both axes, as three kernel-layer kernels with the im2col index math in the loops and no staged matrix: `fprop`, `dgrad` (each input element gathers the taps that reach it, with the divisibility and range test of a strided convolution) and `wgrad`. No IR change: U9 K7 was the stride-1, dilation-1 forward kernel; this generalises it and adds the two backward convolutions CUTLASS names beside it.
