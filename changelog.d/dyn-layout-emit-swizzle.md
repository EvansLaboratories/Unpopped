kind: added: `unpopped-cpu-c` emits `AllocDecl::dyn_layout`; `DynLayout::with_swizzle`
---
Two additive pieces of the dynamic layout (`docs/design-dynamic-layout.md`, section 8 is new). **cpu-c emits a `dyn_layout`**: the physical offset is C arithmetic over the scalar parameters, and the oracle's launch-time proof (shape leaves at least 1, no negative stride, size equal to the extent, every offset inside it, within the 4 Mi cap, no two indices on one offset) is emitted at the top of the kernel function and returns **status 4** exactly where the oracle traps with `KernelTrap::DynLayout`. Declined by name where the layout would be bypassed: a callee, a Device barrier, a peer access, several devices, `Mma`/`MatrixLoad`/`MatrixStore` or a call that passes the array; a struct-field parameter is not spelled. **A swizzle on a `DynLayout`**: `DynLayout::with_swizzle`/`swizzle` (canonical text `swz(B,M,S)(shape:stride)`, unchanged for a layout without one); `check_kernel` checks the rules that need no value (base at least the element width, `align_bytes` a multiple of the period), and the oracle and the C check at the launch that the swizzle's period divides the bound extent (a trap by name, status 4). The swizzle is XOR-bit data and an offset function only: no lookup-table encoding, no tensor-map or descriptor object, no emitted swizzled copy. cpu-c accepts the `align_bytes` a swizzled `dyn_layout` requires (the serial C does not place memory). Patent: pm-kernels' cluster ruling 2026-11-28 (findings not advice, not a clearance): the swizzle hits in `docs/PATENT-SWEEP.md` are outside as read; US20240176663A1 re-read when it grants (the patents lane's row). Tests: the C against the oracle over three layouts and sweeps of argument sets; mutants (each offset term, the injectivity run, the period test, the swizzle XOR at the check and at the access) change the status or the bytes; an exact shift constant of the XOR is not observable through logical accesses (any bijection gives the same bytes), as for the static swizzle.
