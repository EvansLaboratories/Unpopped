kind: `unpopped_convert::lift_kernel`, the C-family kernel-body statement spine (ROADMAP U19 slice 1)
---
`lift_kernel(&Frontend, src, name)` walks the CST of one function and builds a `KernelFn`, or declines the whole kernel with a typed `KernelLiftError` (`NotYetLifted` for a later slice, `OutsideIr` for what the IR's own rules refuse, `SyntaxError`, `NoSuchFunction`, `Invalid` when `check_kernel` rejects the result). Covers scalar and buffer parameters, declarations, assignment and compound assignment to locals and buffer elements, `if`/`else`, `while`, counted `for`, `break`/`continue`/`return`, arithmetic, comparisons, `&&`/`||`/`!`/`?:`, casts, and `a[i]` reads (hoisted into `Load`s; a read in a conditionally evaluated operand declines). C's usual arithmetic conversions are made explicit with `Cast`s. An integer `%` is `OutsideIr`: the IR has no integer remainder the oracle defines. Additive; no IR construct is added. Verified by `tests/kernel_body_spine.rs` (each lifted kernel run on the oracle against a Rust reference on distinct data, a decline per construct class, 12 seeded mutants each caught).
